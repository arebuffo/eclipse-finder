    ⚠️ Eye safety

    Use certified ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses for every partial phase — ordinary sunglasses are not safe. Only during the brief total phase (Moon fully covering the Sun) may you look with the naked eye. In Spain the Sun will be very low in the west-northwest: choose a spot with a completely open WNW horizon (sea, plain or hilltop).
